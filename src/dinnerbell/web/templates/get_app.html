You're in the app

Dinnerbell is on your Home Screen. Open it from there.

{#- Turn on notifications goes here (spec 6.15, "The installable app"): the tap iOS requires, shown only inside the installed app. -#}

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. You might need to tap More or scroll to find it.
  4. Tap Add.
  5. Open Dinnerbell from your Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu, the three dots.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. Open Dinnerbell from your Home screen.

The app on your Home Screen keeps its own sign-in, apart from the browser's.
