Each badge climbs one of three ladders of five counts. In each ladder, every count must be higher than the one before it, and none can be above 10,000. Lowering a count awards the tier to anyone who already has the count, quietly: nobody is announced a badge for a change of rules. Raising a count takes nothing away from anyone who already earned the tier.
