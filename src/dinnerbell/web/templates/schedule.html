Schedule

When each meal's daily call goes out, and how long people have to answer and vote. Anyone in the household can change it. Each change is posted in the Dinnerbell channel with your name, and applies from the next call: a call that's already out or scheduled keeps its times, and a call started again uses the new ones. If today's call hasn't gone out yet, it uses the new schedule when it still can; if the new time has already passed today, today's call keeps the old time. Times are in the household's time zone.
