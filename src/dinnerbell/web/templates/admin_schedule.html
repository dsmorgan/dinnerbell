Ranking

How much each reason counts toward a place's score when Dinnerbell picks proposals. 0 turns a reason off.

{% for name, label in weights %} {% endfor %}

Proposals are drawn at random, and a better score makes a place more likely. Randomness sets how far the draw strays from the best scores. At 1, the default, a place 1 point better is about 2.7 times as likely to be picked. Lower sticks closer to the best scores: 0 always picks them, with a coin toss between equals. Higher, up to 3, mixes the picks up more. Vetoes, guests' needs, and the variety window always hold, and a place someone suggested for the call is always in when it fits.

When nobody has a clear claim on the winning pick's role, because more than one person offered or nobody did, the coordinator can choose the way to get it and who does it. If they don't within this time, the first person who offered does it.

Skip a day

Skip one day's call, today or up to a week ahead, before it goes out. Nothing is posted, and anyone can still start that call from the Today page. To end a call that's already out, use Call it off on the call.
